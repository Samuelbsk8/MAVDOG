A LITTLE CLARITY

Before we
get started.

Which program is right for my dog?+

That depends on your goals, your dog’s needs, and your routine. Private sessions let you learn alongside your dog; Board & Train combines training with overnight care. If you’re unsure, choose “Help me choose” in the inquiry form.

Can I get guidance from home?+

Yes. A virtual consultation lets you discuss training goals and get practical guidance from home.

What should I include in my inquiry?+

Tell us your dog’s name and age, the behaviors or skills you’d like to work on, and which program interests you. It’s fine if you’re still figuring that out.
